## NothingEats: order everything, eat nothing

A fake food-ordering shop with real accounts, mails, search, Kafka events, an AI coach and a courier you can watch on a map. Nothing is charged and nothing arrives. Every number here comes from real visitors who agreed to analytics: the browser (posthog-js) and nine services (server SDKs, same person) report into one project.

Code: [github.com/slashncode/nothingeats](https://github.com/slashncode/nothingeats) · this dashboard as code: [nothingeats-posthog](https://github.com/slashncode/nothingeats-posthog)
